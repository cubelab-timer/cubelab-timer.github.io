Timing a solve

  1. Pick the puzzle in the Event menu. The scramble and a preview of the scrambled puzzle appear at the top.
  2. Scramble your puzzle as shown.
  3. Hold Space. When the time turns from red to green, you are ready. On phones, hold the screen.
  4. Release to start. Press any key (or tap the screen) to stop.
  5. Afterwards you can add +2 or DNF if needed.

You can change how long you need to hold (0.3 s, 0.55 s or none) below the timer.

Cancelling a start

Changed your mind while holding? Press Esc or any key other than Space. On phones, slide your finger off the pad while holding. If inspection is running, the countdown simply continues.

Inspection

Turn on “15s inspection” to get 15 seconds of inspection before each solve, just like at a competition. Press and release Space to start the countdown; going over 15 s adds +2 automatically, and over 17 s gives a DNF.

ao5, ao12 and breakdowns

ao5 is the average of your last 5 solves after dropping the fastest and slowest one, the same rule as WCA averages. ao12 drops one fastest and one slowest out of 12. A DNF counts as the slowest time.

Click any average with a dotted underline to open its breakdown: which solves were counted, which were dropped (in parentheses), and every scramble. “Copy as text” makes it easy to share.

The Stats tab also shows averages up to ao1000, a progress chart, your time distribution, and a goal check such as “10 of 12 under 15 s”.

BPA, WPA and targets

Once you have 4 solves, the timer shows what the next solve can do to your ao5: BPA (best possible average, if the next solve is your fastest) and WPA (worst possible average, if it is a DNF). Enter a target ao5 and it tells you the time you need on the next solve, just like a competition final solve.

2-player battles

Two people race on one computer with the same scramble. The left player uses Left Shift or A, the right player Right Shift or L. On a tablet, each player holds their own panel. Results build up on the Stats tab.

Comparing with WCA results

On the WCA tab, enter your WCA ID (e.g. 2015TANA01) and fetch your results. For each event you see your official personal bests next to your practice bests, with the difference. Without an ID you can still compare with national and world records. Data comes from the public WCA API.

Moving records and backups

The Import/Backup tab has step-by-step guides.

  • From csTimer: in csTimer press EXPORT → “Export to file”, then choose that file here. You can tick only the sessions you want.
  • Back to csTimer: press “Export for csTimer” and import the file in csTimer.
  • New phone or computer: export a backup and import it on the new device.

Importing the same file twice never creates duplicates, and the timer keeps an automatic snapshot of the previous day inside your browser so you can undo mistakes.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, every feature is free. The site is supported by affiliate links and by people who choose to support development.

Where are my records stored?

Only in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers, which also means clearing your browser data deletes them. Export a backup now and then.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

Is this an official WCA tool?

No. It is an unofficial tool run by an individual and is not affiliated with the World Cube Association.
