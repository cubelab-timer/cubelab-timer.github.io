About this site

CubeLab Timer is a free timer for recording and analyzing speedcubing practice. It is made for everyone from new cubers to people who compete, and it will always work without signing up.

Bug reports, feature ideas and event requests are welcome at the contact address above. English and Japanese are both fine.

Relationship with the WCA

This is an unofficial tool run by an individual and is not affiliated with the World Cube Association (WCA). WCA results are fetched from the WCA's public API.

Ads and affiliate links

To cover running costs, this site uses affiliate links (Amazon Associates). As an Amazon Associate, the operator earns from qualifying purchases. No ads are shown while you are timing a solve.

Disclaimer

Times and statistics are calculated carefully, but their accuracy is not guaranteed. Records are stored in your browser and can be lost through browser settings or actions, so export a backup of important records regularly.
